# Verified recognition and official sources

## National second place — Software, 2022

The official TÜBİTAK 2022 national-final results list (now available through a working archived copy because the original URL returns 404) identifies **HÜSEYİN EREN ARIKAN** of İstanbul Özel Saint Michel Fransız Lisesi with advisor **VOLKAN GÖNEN** and the project **"Zeytin Dal Kanserinin Görüntü İşleme ile Teşhisi ve Bacillus Subtilis ile Tedavisi"**.

- Field: **YAZILIM** (Software)
- National final result: **İKİNCİLİK ÖDÜLÜ** (second-place award)
- Listed at item **35** in the final results
- Source: [archived copy of the 2022 TÜBİTAK official national-final results, PDF](https://web.archive.org/web/20240520125501id_/https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2750/final_sonuc_web_son.pdf)

## Regional recognition

A supplied award graphic under TÜBİTAK and İstanbul Technical University branding reads **"Yazılım Dalı Birincisi"** — first place in the software field. This is described as **regional first place** and is separate from the confirmed national second-place result.

## National competition and presidential ceremony

TÜBİTAK reports **25,095 submissions** and **37,283 participating students** in the 2022 competition. The national final included **216 projects**, and the prize ceremony was held at the **Presidential Complex** on **2 June 2022**, in the presence of President **Recep Tayyip Erdoğan**.

The [TÜBİTAK ceremony report](https://tubitak.gov.tr/tr/haber/53-lise-ogrencileri-arastirma-projeleri-yarismasinin-kazananlari-odullerini-cumhurbaskani-erdoganin-elinden-aldi) distinguishes award presenters: **first-place winners** received awards directly from President Erdoğan; **second-place winners** and other awardees received theirs from ministers and TÜBİTAK officials. The project is therefore described as **honoured at a presidential ceremony**, not as personally receiving a second-place trophy from the President without separate individual documentary proof.

## International pathway: Regeneron ISEF

The same official TÜBİTAK report explains that national award-winning projects undergo a further selection to determine which projects represent Türkiye at **Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair (ISEF)** and the European Union Contest for Young Scientists.

The competition's international pathway is established. **This document does not claim an ISEF finalist position, international award, or on-site participation without supporting records.**

## Research figure

The project's original poster reports an image-classification score of **0.88333 (88.33%)** for its four-stage classification model and a four-month study of 12 olive saplings across four groups. Those are self-reported research results, not independently validated deployment metrics.
